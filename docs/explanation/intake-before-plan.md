# Intake before plan

Planning asks “how will we change the system?” Intake asks “what outcome did the person actually ask us to establish?”

AI workflows often decompose too early. A plausible plan appears before language, success conditions, unresolved business rules, or expected proof are captured. More planning does not repair a wrong target.

The outcome contract creates a small boundary before execution. It records the question, success checks, required answers, state, and evidence references so the eventual plan can be checked against what was requested.

The goal is not a long interview. Inspect first. Ask only questions whose answers can materially change scope, risk, evidence, or execution. Senior users need fewer, sharper questions, not beginner questionnaires with more jargon.
