# Harness, platform and market practice for verified data science (research note C2, 2026-09-12)

## Summary
BrotherDS checks claims before a business decision by running deterministic gates that return PASS, FAIL or NO-DATA, recording HELD/MISSED/UNSCOREABLE when reality arrives, scoring quantile forecasts and feeding lessons to shared memory. This note maps how the market already expresses that idea and where BrotherDS should stay stricter. Alibaba DataWorks, Huawei DataArts and Tencent WeData all use rule templates with strong versus weak severity and blocking of downstream tasks versus alert only. Western MDM and lakehouse vendors measure duplicate rate, completeness, hierarchy validity and match precision but disclose little about evaluation method. Snowflake and Databricks tie AI answers to verified SQL and trusted assets. Agent harnesses use tests as oracles inside Docker sandboxes. Observability adds freshness, volume, schema, distribution and lineage. Forecast scoring uses weighted interval score which approximates CRPS. The recommendations turn these patterns into gates.

## 1. LLMs as matchers and as labellers for entity resolution (what agreement with human reviewers looks like, calibration, where they fail)

LLMs can act in two roles that BrotherDS must separate. As matchers they decide if two records refer to the same entity. As labellers they generate training or evaluation labels that must be calibrated against blind human review.

The most rigorous labeller workflow in the fetched pages is Potato Judge Calibration [1]. It runs each LLM k times per item, takes the modal label and uses vote fraction across k samples as empirical confidence, keeps LLM labels in a separate store invisible to humans, then samples a blind human label set and reports human versus LLM accuracy, Cohen kappa and Fleiss kappa, Krippendorff alpha, expected calibration error with reliability bins and Brier score, plus confusion matrices [1]. Temperature must be greater than 0 or confidence is always 1.0 and calibration is meaningless [1]. For span tasks it clusters spans by IoU at least 0.5 and notes the method is heuristic and directional only [1].

General guidance on human evaluation design emphasizes calibration sessions before launch, honeypot injection of 5 to 10 percent gold items with a target of at least 85 percent gold accuracy, and tracking time per item and flagging annotators below 50 percent of median time [2]. Agreement is interpreted as kappa below 0.20 poor, 0.21 to 0.40 fair, 0.41 to 0.60 moderate, 0.61 to 0.80 substantial, 0.81 to 1.00 almost perfect, with Krippendorff alpha at least 0.80 reliable and 0.67 to 0.80 tentatively acceptable [2]. Pairwise comparison gives higher agreement than Likert at lower cognitive load, and a 4 point scale without neutral reduces central tendency bias [2]. The hybrid triage pattern is to route auto high confidence first, then LLM judge, then humans, which can cut human volume by 70 to 90 percent [2]. The page advises recalibrating the judge when human versus LLM agreement drops below 0.7 [2]. This page is practice guidance with example code, not a controlled experiment, so treat its cost and agreement claims as directional rather than evidence.

For matching performance, Peeters et al. 2025 evaluated GPT-4, GPT-4o, GPT-4o mini, Llama2 70B, Llama3.1 70B and Mixtral 8x7B on six benchmarks including WDC Products, Abt-Buy, Walmart-Amazon, Amazon-Google, DBLP-Scholar and DBLP-ACM with careful serialization and parsing for yes [3][4]. In zero shot, GPT-4 outperforms all other LLMs on all product datasets by at least 1 percent F1 and achieves 89 percent or higher F1 on 5 of 6 datasets with no task specific training data, while GPT-4o is 0.1 to 1 percent lower on publication data and 1 to 3 percent lower on products [3]. GPT-4o mini is up to 6 percent worse than GPT-4o [3]. Llama3.1 consistently beats Llama2 by 1 to 21 percent F1 and is comparable to GPT-4o mini when the right prompt is chosen, Mixtral lags by 7 to 16 percent on 4 datasets [3]. No single prompt is best across models or datasets, the best prompt depends on model and dataset combination [3]. Prompt sensitivity measured as standard deviation over 10 prompts is lowest for GPT-4 at mean 2.26, versus 6.18 to 18.54 for other models [3].

In context learning helps in about 61 percent of model and dataset combinations but hurts GPT-4 on WDC Products and Abt-Buy and causes 4 to 26 percent drops for GPT-4o mini and Mixtral, so usefulness must be tested per pair [3]. Versus fine tuned PLMs, GPT-4 zero shot beats the best PLM baseline by 2.65 to 4.71 percent F1 on three of six datasets and is within 0.33 to 4.49 percent lower on the other three [3]. Transfer of fine tuned RoBERTa and Ditto to unseen WDC Products drops by 22 to 61 percent and 36 to 56 percent respectively, while all LLMs are at least 8 percent higher than the best transferred PLM and GPT-4 is 40 to 68 percent higher, indicating robustness to unseen entities without fine tuning [3]. GPT-4 can also generate structured explanations and error classes for wrong decisions [3][4].

Failed retrieval of the EDBT PDF body [5] means we rely on the arXiv HTML and abstract for numbers, note the PDF is not usable evidence here.

Implication for BrotherDS. Do not accept an LLM label or match as PASS without a blind human sample and calibration report. Require k greater than 1 with temperature greater than 0, report ECE, kappa and confusion, and enforce that missing human overlap is NO-DATA, not a pass. For entity resolution, treat an LLM matcher as NO-DATA unless blocked evaluation shows precision, recall and F1 on a held out set disjoint at the entity level, because PLM style leakage inflates scores on seen entities.

## 2. Chinese data platforms: how Alibaba DataWorks, Huawei DataArts Studio and Tencent WeData express data quality rules and monitoring (rule templates, thresholds, blocking versus warning rules)

All three platforms share the same pattern described above for BrotherDS blocking semantics. A rule template plus threshold plus severity decides whether a failure blocks downstream tasks or only alerts.

Alibaba DataWorks defines Template as validation logic, Monitoring Rule as a template applied to a table or field with a threshold, and Monitor as the execution plan that binds rules to a scheduling node and triggers on node success [6][7][8]. Built in templates cover table row count and distinct count and field level checks, plus custom template and custom SQL where the SQL must return a single numeric value [6][8]. Comparison methods are explicit. Numeric type compares a result to a fixed expected value with Greater Than, Greater Than or Equal To, Equal To, Unequal To, Less Than, Less Than or Equal To and supports normal threshold and critical threshold [6][8]. Fluctuation compares range with Absolute Value, Raise and Drop and adds a warning threshold plus critical threshold [6][8]. Intelligent dynamic threshold uses a system algorithm to set the threshold automatically and supports strong and weak rules [6][8]. Only custom SQL, custom range and dynamic threshold support dynamic thresholds [6][8]. Each node monitors only one table, non partitioned tables monitor the whole table, partitioned tables need a partition filter expression such as ds equals scheduling parameter, and quality nodes run only on serverless resource groups [6][8]. The handling policy distinguishes strong rule check failed, strong rule critical exception, strong rule warning exception, weak rule check failed, weak rule critical exception and weak rule warning exception [6][8]. The policy is Do not ignore which stops the current node and sets Failed so downstream nodes do not execute, or Ignore which continues downstream [6][8]. Notifications are email, SMS, phone, DingTalk, WeCom, Lark or custom webhook [6][7][8]. Retain problem data can automatically create a table with offending rows for MaxCompute and Hologres on some rules [6][8].

Huawei DataArts Quality is described as monitoring offline data, verifying when offline data changes and blocking the production link to avoid spread, plus managing historical verification results for analysis and grading [9][10]. Its perspectives are integrity, validity, timeliness, consistency, accuracy and uniqueness, and it can filter unqualified data in single column or across columns, rows, sources and tables [10]. It can auto generate standardized rules from data standards in DataArts Architecture and periodically monitor [10]. Modules are Dashboard with counts of jobs, instances, anomaly tables and alarm trends, Rule Template with built in and custom templates, Quality Job that applies templates or custom rules to tables, Comparison Job that applies rules to two existing tables and outputs comparison, O and M Management and Quality Report [10]. The fetched Huawei pages are brief product docs dated 2024, they state blocking and dimensions but give no template list or threshold math, so they are weaker evidence than Alibaba and Tencent for gate design.

Tencent WeData repeats the same core [11][12][13]. It supports Tencent engines EMR, DLC, TCHouse-P, TCHouse-D plus Doris, and supports table and field level rules with execution policies and rule strength that determines blocking of downstream tasks [12][13]. Rule Template offers 56 plus system built in templates view only and custom CRUD templates [12]. Quality supports two execution modes. Independent cycle runs periodic inspection on tables with daily, hourly or minute frequency and notifies on exception. Associated Scheduling associates a quality task with a production task for data sync or development and inserts validation after the production task completes, blocking downstream on failure based on task level [12]. Quality scoring is computed from six dimensions accuracy, timeliness, integrity, uniqueness, consistency and validity to produce library and table level reports [12]. Notifications cover WeCom Group, WeChat, call, SMS, mail, Lark and DingTalk with costs split into edition fee, execution resource fee and engine compute cost [11][12][13]. The product overview pages [11][13] are marketing, use slogans like up to 50 percent lower development time without methodology, and should not be cited for effectiveness. Use the documentation page [12] for factual features.

For BrotherDS this validates the strong versus weak and blocking versus warning distinction as market standard. BrotherDS should keep it but make thresholds auditable with explicit normal, warning and critical bands and require the partition or scope expression in the claim JSON, otherwise NO-DATA.

## 4. Semantic layers and verified queries (Snowflake Cortex Analyst, Databricks Genie): how they keep an AI answer tied to trusted definitions

Both Snowflake and Databricks use the same idea that matters for BrotherDS. Keep a semantic model with logical tables and columns, synonyms and verified example SQL, then check whether an AI answer comes from trusted definitions or is free text guessing.

Snowflake Cortex Analyst Verified Query Repository stores a collection of human verified questions with corresponding SQL that uses logical table and column names defined in the semantic model, prefixed with double underscores in the SQL, not the underlying physical names [21][22]. Fields tracked per verified query include name, question, verified_at as seconds since epoch, verified_by, use_as_onboarding_question and sql [21][22]. The semantic model also defines dimensions, time dimensions with synonyms like time_of_sale and transaction_time, and measures like profit defined as amt minus cst with synonyms earnings and net income [21][22]. Cortex Analyst then leverages relevant verified SQL when answering similar questions and can show which verified query was used via the confidence field in the API response [21][22]. An open source Streamlit semantic model generator helps add and verify queries by cloning the generator repo, providing credentials, chatting to generate SQL, inspecting results, editing until correct and saving as verified query [21][22]. The optimization feature analyzes verified queries to generalize semantic concepts, for example inferring an is_active filter definition from a verified query about active users last month, with more than 20 verified queries noted as slower because each verified query may be executed up to four times [20]. The guidance warns that invalid or inaccurate verified queries negatively impact accuracy [21][22].

Databricks Genie uses Trusted Assets for the same purpose [23][24][25]. A Trusted Asset is a verified query or function that Genie executes exactly as defined, without adaptation, versus regular SQL examples which may be adapted [24]. Trusted assets are added in the Instructions panel via Add trusted asset as either a parameterized example SQL query or a SQL function with natural language description, and responses that use them show a Trusted badge that shifts the interaction from chatting with AI to viewing official data [24]. Authors are advised to reserve Trusted Assets for regulatory metrics, executive KPIs like year to date revenue and formulas with specific tax or discount rules, keeping the badge meaningful [24]. For evaluation, Genie provides Benchmarks up to 500 questions per agent [23]. Benchmark questions support Chat mode which compares SQL result sets and Agent mode which uses an LLM judge with an optional evaluation note [23]. Databricks recommends 2 to 4 phrasings per question and including a SQL answer as ground truth [23][25]. Chat mode rates Good when result sets match exactly or with same data sorted differently or numeric values rounding to 4 significant digits, and Bad when result is empty or error or has extra columns or different single cell value, with truncation at 5,000 rows noted as a cause of mismatches [23]. Feedback mechanisms include Is this correct, Fix it and Request review which flags for manual review without automatically changing behavior [23][25]. Monitoring includes weekly digest, Analyze Space Usage with citations and full conversation review when sharing is set to Reviewable by agent managers [23].

For BrotherDS the pattern is directly reusable. A claim that an AI answer is correct must point to a verified query or trusted asset identifier with verified_at and verified_by, otherwise NO-DATA. No claim should PASS on an unverified semantic definition.

## 5. Agent harnesses West and China (SWE-agent, OpenHands, Qwen-Agent, DeepSeek and others the pages cover): the verification loop, tests as oracles, what makes an agent's claim trustworthy

A trustworthy agent claim is one where the harness made the environment legible to the model, constrained its actions, ran tests as the oracle and logged everything.

SWE-agent and OpenHands share an Agent Computer Interface design philosophy that the interface matters more than the model [28][29][27]. SWE-agent with GPT-4o and a good ACI outperforms the same model with raw bash, with ablation improvements of minus 15 percent token waste from truncation, plus 8 percent fix rate from highlighting errors, plus 12 percent accuracy from dedicated edit_file and plus 10 percent comprehension from clean markdown [28]. SWE-agent defines a tight action space of open_file, edit_file, search_dir, run_command and submit_patch, deliberately omitting create_file and delete_file and forcing old string to new string replacement to keep edits auditable [28]. ACI is YAML configurable [28]. The maintenance note says SWE-agent is now maintenance only superseded by mini-swe-agent but still preferred when rich ACIs are needed [27].

OpenHands is the heavyweight production harness. It is a composable Python SDK with four packages openhands.sdk, openhands.tools, openhands.workspace and openhands.agent_server, MIT licensed, model agnostic via LiteLLM with 100 plus providers, native MCP, bash and file editing tools, condenser for context compression, sub agent delegation and local to Docker to remote workspace portability with REST and WebSocket server [27]. It tops SWE-bench Verified on CodeAct and was used by GLM-5.2 on SWE-bench Pro [27][28]. The architecture isolates each session in a fresh ephemeral Ubuntu Docker sandbox with preinstalled Python, Node, Rust, Go, Java, package managers, gcc, git and Chromium so the agent cannot harm the host and starts deterministic [28]. The Agent Canvas browser UI offers code editor, terminal, browser and chat with modes Autonomous, Suggest with approval and Chat steering [28]. Cost strategy shows tiering matters, simpler tasks on Haiku around 0.03 to 0.05 dollars versus Sonnet 0.60 to 0.80 dollars per task [28]. Benchmarks are run via a standardized harness with rich logging and Python stack, available benchmarks include SWE-Bench, SWE-Bench Pro, GAIA, Commit0, OpenAgentSafety and ProgramBench [26].

OpenHands provides benchmark evaluation infrastructure with docker workspace default and remote workspace via runtime API for massive parallelization, requiring prebuilt images tagged by SDK SHA and RUNTIME_API_KEY [26]. The model-agnostic claim and harness optimization surface are repeated across harnesses.

Qwen-Agent is the Chinese counterpart described as framework built on Qwen with function calling, MCP, Code Interpreter, RAG and Chrome extension, now the backend of Qwen Chat, Apache 2.0 licensed with 17.1k stars [29][30]. It offers atomic components LLMs inheriting BaseChatModel and Tools inheriting BaseTool plus Agents inheriting Agent, with default parallel multi step multi turn tool calls and nous template for Qwen3 [29][30]. Its Code Interpreter runs Python inside Docker containers with Jupyter kernels, mounting only the configured work_dir and providing isolated execution with text, image and error outputs, with five TCP ports allocated and connection files separated for host and container [31][32]. Timeout uses a custom _M6CountdownTimer and ANSI escapes are stripped, images are served via M6_CODE_INTERPRETER_STATIC_URL when run via the server [31][32]. The disclaimer notes basic sandbox isolation that should be used with caution in production [29][30]. This matches the loopengineering advice to never auto merge without human review.

DeepSeek evaluation must be read carefully. DeepWiki pages detail benchmarks for DeepSeek-V3 Base 671B with 37B activated and DeepSeek-V3 Chat, with evaluation across MMLU, DROP, HumanEval, MBPP, CRUXEval, GSM8K, MATH, CMMLU and others, showing best on 22 of 26 base benchmarks and chat results like MMLU 88.5, MATH-500 90.2, LiveCodeBench 40.5 with chain of thought and Arena-Hard 85.5 [33][34]. The generic DeepSeek-LLM evaluation page covers HellaSwag, PIQA, WinoGrande, TriviaQA, BBH, Hungarian High School exam and LeetCode contests, with findings that multiple choice training inflates MMLU, so the team chose not to optimize for it [34]. These are model capability benchmarks, not harness verification.

Elser AI analysis of DeepSeek V4 Pro adds the harness caution [35]. Vendor reports 87.9 on Terminal Bench 2.1, 61.5 on NL2Repo, 62.7 on DeepSWE and 74.1 on Toolathlon-Verified [35]. The page states these are first party claims, not independent reproduction, notes DSBench-FullStack and DSBench-Hard are internal evaluations, and explains that score reflects the system around the model including harness, temperature, thinking effort, token budget, tool descriptions and time limits, with DeepSeek disclosure of minimal harness mode, max effort, top p 0.95 temperature 1.0 for public tests [35]. It recommends evaluation with 30 to 100 real backlog tasks, defining success as compile, pass tests, satisfy issue, avoid unrelated changes and cite files, and measuring wall clock time, cost, tool calls, unnecessary changes, introduced failures, review time, destructive attempts and variance [35]. This page is explicit that marketing language like greatly enhances is not measurement.

For BrotherDS, an agent claim is trustworthy only when it shows the Docker image or sandbox identity, the exact tool and ACI config, the test suite as oracle with pass and fail counts, and a trajectory log with edit diff. Without those it is NO-DATA. BrotherDS lessons should record harness and prompt sensitivity, since the same model can shift 6 to 18 points of F1 across prompts.

## 6. Observability and lineage (Monte Carlo, OpenLineage): freshness, volume, schema, lineage facets

Observability answers is the data wrong before stakeholders notice. The discipline is summarized as shrinking data downtime, the gap between data break and someone noticing, where no job fails but numbers drift [36][37]. Datadef frames five pillars freshness is data on time, volume expected row counts, schema breaking changes, distribution values in expected range, lineage source and impact mapping, with tools dbt tests, Great Expectations, Soda for predefined rules and Monte Carlo, Bigeye, Metaplane for ML anomaly detection [36]. Monte Carlo style detection learns normal from history and alerts on deviation per hour of week versus writing one rule per table, with a simple SQL primitive of max updated_at, staleness and count per table compared to its own history [37]. Implementation roadmap suggests phase 1 freshness and volume on top 10 critical tables in weeks 1 to 2, phase 2 schema and distribution in weeks 3 to 4, phase 3 lineage and incident response in month 2 tracking mean time to detect and mean time to resolve [36]. Shirokoff details detection versus tests, tests catch known unknowns you thought to predict, anomaly detection catches unknown unknowns by profiling metadata over time, and neither alone is enough [37]. Alert fatigue is called the killer, a real but ignored alert is worse than no alert, so routing by severity, suppressing seasonal patterns and tying to ownership is the product [37]. Lineage is the multiplier, detection says a table is wrong, lineage says which upstream caused it and which downstream is contaminated, the blast radius, without lineage every incident is manual archaeology [37].

The Monte Carlo evaluation guide PDF reference [38] was fetched as a PDF that could not be fully extracted as text, its title indicates vendor evaluation guidance, treat as marketing evaluation aid rather than independent measurement.

OpenLineage provides the open standard for lineage collection [39][40][41]. It defines an event model with eventType, eventTime, run with runId, job with namespace and name, inputs and outputs as datasets and producer [39][40]. Extensibility is via Facets, atomic metadata attached to Run, Job or Dataset, each with _producer and _schemaURL that must be an immutable versioned URL, with naming pattern prefix plus name plus entity Facet PascalCased and key prefix underscore name snake_case, promoted custom facets can become standard [39][40]. Standard facets referenced include schema with fields, sql for query, parent run and nominal time [41]. Example lineage events show inputs workshop.public.taxes-in and outputs workshop.public.taxes-out with schema facets and job sql facet insert into taxes_out select id name is_active from taxes_in [39]. Custom facets are payload as is and are stored if they contain mandatory fields, even if not standard, enabling integration specific metadata like Airflow runArgs and airflow version [41]. The reference implementation is Marquez [39].

For BrotherDS, freshness, volume, schema and distribution are detection gates that can be computed from warehouse metadata without business logic, while lineage facets provide the provenance a HELD or MISSED outcome needs later. Any claim about a table being ready should carry freshness staleness, row count versus history band and schema hash, otherwise NO-DATA.

## 7. Forecast evaluation (weighted interval score and calibration)

When forecasts are issued as median plus central prediction intervals, as in COVID-19 Forecast Hub with median plus 10 percent to 98 percent intervals corresponding to 23 quantiles [42][44], the proper score is the weighted interval score which approximates the continuous ranked probability score [42][44].

The interval score for a single interval at level alpha is interval width plus 2 divided by alpha times distance below lower bound if observed below, plus 2 divided by alpha times distance above upper bound if observed above, otherwise just width [42][44]. It decomposes into dispersion or sharpness plus overprediction plus underprediction penalties [43][44]. The weighted interval score for K intervals plus median is the weighted average of interval scores plus absolute error for the median. Specification from Bracher et al uses weights w0 equals 1 half for median and wk equals alpha k divided by 2 for each interval, so that for large K and equally spaced alpha stretching over unit interval the WIS converges to CRPS [42][44]. In that setup with K equals 0 the WIS equals absolute error, and with point forecasts CRPS also reduces to absolute error, so scores are on the natural data scale [42][43][44]. A version that counts median twice weights it like an interval rather than a single quantile and is also valid, controlled by count_median_twice in scoringutils [43].

Empirical illustration uses a negative binomial forecast with expectation 60 and dispersion 4 and a competing sharper forecast expectation 80 size 10, showing WIS is distance sensitive while log score is local and can favor the more dispersed forecast when the observation is in the tail, for example y equals 190 gives log scores minus 9.37 versus minus 9.69 but WIS 103.9 versus 87.8 [42]. On FluSight 2016/2017 26 models averaged over weeks and horizons, WIS correlates strongly with CRPS and absolute error and almost perfectly under 23 quantiles, while log score agreement is mediocre because too sharp forecasts are severely punished by log score but less so by interval scores [42]. This supports using WIS for unknown predictability contexts where tail observations are common.

For intervals to improve future coverage, evaluation should also assess calibration. Bracher et al describe probability integral transform histograms which should be uniform for calibrated forecasts, with L shape downward bias, J shape upward bias, U shape under dispersion and inverse U over dispersion, and notes that with quantile format exact PIT cannot be computed but decile occupancy and handling of ties by splitting counts can approximate it [42]. The scoringutils page gives usage wis observed, predicted, quantile_level with arguments weigh and count_median_twice and components dispersion_quantile, overprediction_quantile and underprediction_quantile [43].

For BrotherDS, store quantile forecasts as ordered levels with predicted quantiles, score with WIS using w equals alpha over 2 and report dispersion, over and under components separately plus empirical coverage per interval level. Do not score log score from quantiles alone without tail assumptions.

## Recommendations

1. **Require a blind human calibration sample for any LLM label or match claim.** WHY: Potato shows labeling must keep LLMs blind and report ECE, kappa, alpha and confusion or calibration is meaningless, with temperature greater than 0 and k greater than 1 [1]. Guidance flags agreement below 0.7 as retune threshold and uses honeypots and time monitoring for quality [2]. LLM matching gains of 40 to 68 percent over transferred PLMs still depend on prompt and dataset fit [3]. Lands in tool as **new gate: llm_judge_calibration**. Cost of not doing it is inflated precision and silent false merges that pass review because no human overlap exists to contradict the model.

2. **Enforce prompt sensitivity testing per model and dataset, do not fix one prompt.** WHY: No single best prompt exists and standard deviation is 2.26 for GPT-4 versus 6.18 to 18.54 for others, so the same prompt can swing 20 plus points of F1 [3]. Qwen and DeepSeek disclosures show harness and sampling settings change benchmark outcomes [33][35]. Lands in tool as **new gate: prompt_sensitivity_check** that reruns at least 5 prompt variants and fails the claim if std exceeds tolerance. Cost is a model that looks great on report prompt but fails on deployment phrasing.

3. **Adopt strong versus weak severity with explicit blocking behavior.** WHY: Alibaba and Tencent both define strong rules that block downstream and weak rules that only alert, with thresholds normal versus warning versus critical [6][12]. Huawei also blocks production link on verification failure [9][10]. BrotherDS already intercepts dirty data, this makes the policy explicit and auditable. Lands in tool as **existing: strong_weak_blocking** extend with exception categories strong rule critical and weak rule warning. Cost is either blocking nothing important or blocking everything including benign drift.

4. **Require rule template identifier, scope expression and threshold math in the claim JSON.** WHY: Alibaba requires template selection, rule scope table or field, comparison method Numeric versus Fluctuation and thresholds normal plus error plus warning where applicable [6][8]. Custom SQL must return single numeric value and include partition filter [6][8]. Without this the gate cannot be deterministic. Lands in tool as **new gate: rule_contract_completeness** returning NO-DATA if template, scope, comparison method or partition filter missing. Cost is rules that pass because no check was actually defined.

5. **Score quantile forecasts with weighted interval score with convergence weighting and keep components separate.** WHY: Bracher defines WIS with w0 equals 0.5 and wk equals alpha k over 2 converging to CRPS and decomposing into dispersion plus over and under penalties [42][44]. scoringutils implements this and warns median counted twice changes weighting [43]. Lands in tool as **existing: quantile_forecast** replace any ad hoc interval averaging with WIS and report dispersion, overprediction and underprediction. Cost is tail sensitive business risk hidden by averaging interval widths alone.

6. **Require semantic tying for any AI generated answer claim via verified query or trusted asset.** WHY: Snowflake requires verified_at, verified_by and verified SQL on logical names for Cortex Analyst to be trustworthy and warns invalid verified queries hurt accuracy [21][22]. Databricks distinguishes Trusted Assets that run exactly as defined from regular examples that may be adapted and shows Trusted badge only when used [24][23]. Lands in tool as **new gate: verified_semantic_tie** checking verified query identifier exists and confidence field references it. Cost is persuasive but wrong answers that cite a table that was never approved.

7. **Treat AI MDM vendor KPI lifts as marketing until reproduced on your data.** WHY: AIpedia claims plus 40 points quality and minus 80 percent stewardship without method [15]. Informatica leader quote is press release [14]. Profisee and Zingg pages describe features without evaluation numbers [16][18][19]. StackBriefly advises piloting with messy real data including duplicates and source priority conflicts and measuring duplicate reduction, completeness and matching precision [17]. Lands in tool as **docs only** guidance for claim evaluation, and **existing: master_data** add mandatory pilot metrics. Cost is buying a platform on leaderboard hype and missing entity level generalization.

8. **Make the agent verification loop explicit with Docker sandbox, exact ACI and tests as oracle.** WHY: LoopEngineering shows ACI fixes give 5 to 15 percent gains and that Docker sandbox prevents host damage and gives reproducibility [28]. OpenHands requires sandbox per session and versioned images [26][28]. Qwen Code Interpreter mounts only work_dir and still warns cautions for production [29][31]. Elser notes agent scores reflect harness limits [35]. Lands in tool as **new gate: agent_verification_loop** requiring container image SHA, action space definition, test command and log. Cost is patches that succeed in transcript but break final state and cannot be audited.

9. **Emit freshness, volume, schema and distribution checks before any business content claim, and attach lineage facets.** WHY: Five pillars define freshness volume schema distribution as detection and lineage as diagnosis and blast radius [36][37]. Simple SQL of max updated_at and count versus hour of week history is the primitive [37]. OpenLineage requires events with runId, job namespace and name, inputs and outputs plus facets with _producer and _schemaURL [39][40][41]. Lands in tool as **new gate: observability_freshness_volume_schema** and **existing: golden_record_lineage** adding OpenLineage facets. Cost is eleven day silent drift where job succeeded but currency field changed and no gate fired.

10. **Control alert fatigue by routing, ownership and suppression, not by adding more monitors.** WHY: Monitoring guides state that alert quality is the product, a muted channel detects nothing and real but ignored alerts are worse than none, with advice to suppress seasonal patterns and tie alerts to owners [37][36]. DataWorks and WeData support multiple notification channels and severity routing [6][12]. Lands in tool as **existing: pipeline_quality** add suppression rules and ownership check. Cost is credibility loss when every Monday volume anomaly pages oncall and operators mute the system.

11. **Record entity disjoint splits for any match or deduplication evaluation.** WHY: Peeters shows transferred PLMs drop 22 to 61 percent on unseen entities while LLMs hold [3]. MDM selection guidance emphasizes testing with real duplicates and inactive records [17]. Without disjoint entities, reported F1 is optimistic. Lands in tool as **new gate: entity_disjoint_evaluation**. Cost is a matcher that scores 0.9 in lab but collapses on new products.

12. **Report calibration for quantile forecasts via PIT histograms and coverage per level, not just mean WIS.** WHY: Bracher notes PIT uniformity, L, J, U and inverse U shapes and approximation when only quantiles exist [42]. Calibration tells fairness of intervals beyond average score. Lands in tool as **new gate: quantile_calibration** requiring coverage at each alpha. Cost is sharp intervals that look good on WIS but are systematically overconfident.

13. **Version every rule, threshold and verified query, and store lesson memory when reality arrives.** WHY: BrotherDS promise is HELD, MISSED, UNSCOREABLE scored later and lessons fed back. Market practice in Snowflake verified_at and verified_by and OpenLineage _schemaURL immutability supports this [21][39]. Agent benchmark pages emphasize reproducible SDK commit pinning and image tags [26]. Lands in tool as **existing: shared_memory** add immutable versioned URLs for custom facets and thresholds. Cost is lessons that cannot be replayed because the rule that passed is not the rule now deployed.

## Confidence

**Section 1 LLMs as matchers and labellers: high.** Based on peer reviewed EDBT 2025 paper with full benchmark tables and explicit prompt sensitivity numbers [3][4], plus concrete calibration workflow with metrics and blind labeling [1]. Uncertainty is model cutoff dates and temperature defaults varying across providers.

**Section 2 Chinese data platforms: high for Alibaba and Tencent, medium for Huawei.** Alibaba docs give detailed template, comparison method and policy specifics with screenshots [6][8], Tencent docs give 56 plus templates and scoring dimensions and modes [12]. Huawei docs are brief and do not detail thresholds [9][10], so Huawei specifics are medium confidence.

**Section 3 Western MDM: low to medium.** Informatica press release confirms Leader placement but not method [14]. Profisee and Zingg are feature marketing without evaluation [16][18][19]. AIpedia is affiliate marketing with undisclosed method and should be treated as low confidence [15]. StackBriefly is practical buying guidance with concrete checklist and trade offs, medium confidence [17].

**Section 4 Semantic layers: high.** Snowflake docs specify verified_queries YAML with required fields and note performance behavior [20][21][22]. Databricks docs specify Trusted Assets, result comparison math at 4 digits and 5000 row limit, and benchmark caps of 500 [23][24][25].

**Section 5 Agent harnesses: high for harness mechanics, medium for model score interpretation.** ACI and sandbox mechanics are detailed with numbers for ablation gains and costs [28]. Qwen-Agent Docker code interpreter architecture is explicit [31][32]. DeepSeek vendor scores are first party and harness sensitive, so interpretation confidence is medium [33][35].

**Section 6 Observability and lineage: high for concepts and standard.** Five pillars and primitives and distinction between tests and anomaly detection are consistent across independent guides [36][37]. OpenLineage spec defines facets and event shape precisely [39][40][41]. Monte Carlo PDF extraction was unusable so that source is low confidence [38].

**Section 7 Forecast evaluation: high.** Primary statistical derivation with WIS to CRPS convergence and decomposition is peer reviewed and replicated in scoringutils docs with explicit formulas and code signatures [42][43][44].
