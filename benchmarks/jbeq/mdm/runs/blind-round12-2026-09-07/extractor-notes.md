## General notes on enum mapping and round 9 widening

- object_type enum has no 'supplier' value; commercial_account used as the nearest generic business-partner type where the input names a 仕入先 (supplier) record (EO-05).
- The requirements (RQ) and survivorship (SV) tracks are not entity-merge comparisons; the identity-kind fields (object_type_a/b, lifecycle, hierarchy_parents, authoritative_identifier, location_comparison) mostly stay at schema default for these two tracks and for the temporal (TM) track's non-date fields, since EXTRACTOR-PROMPT.md does not define dedicated semantics for those tracks beyond the general fields. Facts that have no clean field are written out in full above rather than forced into a field that does not fit, per EXTRACTOR-PROMPT.md's closing instruction to extract the fact even when the engine cannot yet act on it.
- Round 9 widening applied literally: requested_action=match_on_stated_basis is set whenever the question itself proposes merging/matching/linking on a named or surrounding-fact basis (AD-01/02/03/04/07/10, EO-02/04/05/06/09, HI-08/09, ID-01/04, MM-02/06/10), OR whenever a matching/deduplication step already presented the two records as a candidate pair on a named basis before an open question was asked (HI-06 name similarity, ID-05 code match, MM-05 the 0.83 score, MM-09 the 0.91 score). It is left at 'none' for genuinely open dispositional questions ('どう扱うべきか' with no named basis anywhere in the passage: AD-05/06/08, EO-01/03/07/08/10, HI-01/03/07, ID-02/03, MM-01/03/04/07/08) and for hierarchy-write requests, which use requested_action=assignment instead (HI-02/04/05/10).
- contradicted_attributes never repeats the primary identifier already captured by stated_difference/authoritative_identifier (e.g. two differing valid corporate numbers); it is reserved for OTHER shared attributes the two records both state and disagree on (a name notation variant, payment terms, a sales rep, prefecture coverage), per rule 4 and the schema's own worked examples.
- Where an input states an identifier's equality without giving its actual value (EO-03, EO-05, MM-03), identifiers stays empty rather than inventing a value; the fact is instead carried by stated_relation and/or evidence_strength/evidence_reasons.
