SCORER ATTRIBUTION FINDING (report engine and direct halves separately, as
briefed): the "engine-decided: 46 of 60" and "direct-answered: 10 of 10"
lines above are the scorer's own literal output, but they misattribute the
5 requirements (RQ) cases to the engine. cmd_score's _engine_decided_ids
(scripts/jbeq_mdm.py) counts a case as engine-decided whenever
decisions.jsonl's rule_fired is not literally "track-unsupported". For
survivorship this is correct: decide()'s track-unsupported branch returns
answer="NO-DATA", and "NO-DATA" IS one of SV's own allowed_answers, so
rule_fired stays "track-unsupported" and the 10 SV cases are correctly
excluded. For requirements, "NO-DATA" is NOT in RQ's own allowed_answers
(["DECIDED", "ASSUMED", "INFERRED", "UNKNOWN"]), so finish()'s
allowed-answers guard fires: it tries a CAUTION_RANK remap, finds none,
and falls back to the literal answer "NO-DATA" with rule_fired rewritten
to "safety-net" (scripts/jbeq_decide.py lines ~1012-1020). That
overwritten rule_fired is what decisions.jsonl records for all five RQ
cases, so _engine_decided_ids's exact-string check never recognizes them
as track-unsupported and folds them into "engine-decided" instead.
Reclassifying RQ correctly (its own track line already shows 3 of 5
correct) gives the numbers the scorer should have printed:
  true engine-decided (the 5 rule-based tracks plus temporal): 43 of 55
  true direct-answered (requirements plus survivorship): 13 of 15
Both halves sum to the same 56 of 70 total correct as the scorer's own
figures (46+10 = 43+13 = 56), so no score is wrong, only which bucket
each case's correctness is filed under. This is a gap in
_engine_decided_ids's track-unsupported detection for any track whose own
allowed_answers excludes "NO-DATA", not an extraction defect; the RQ fact
sheets and RQ direct answers here are unaffected by it.
