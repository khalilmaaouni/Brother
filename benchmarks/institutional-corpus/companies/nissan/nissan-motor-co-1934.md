# Nissan Motor Co., Ltd. (1934 incorporation, current)

Nissan Motor Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 1934 when Yoshisuke Aikawa separated the automobile division of Tobata Casting. This is the current legal name of Nissan as of 2026.

claim: Yoshisuke Aikawa separated the automobile division from Tobata Casting and incorporated it as Nissan Motor Co., Ltd. in 1934, after the Nissan zaibatsu took control of DAT Motors. [evidence: n-70f20d90]
