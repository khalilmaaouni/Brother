# Jidosha-Seizo Co., Ltd. (1933 rename)

Renamed Jidosha-Seizo Co., Ltd. in 1933 and relocated to Yokohama, immediately before Yoshisuke Aikawa's 1934 incorporation of Nissan Motor Co., Ltd.
