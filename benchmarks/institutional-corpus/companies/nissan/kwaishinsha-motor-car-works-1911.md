# Kwaishinsha Motor Car Works (1911)

Nissan's earliest predecessor. Kwaishinsha produced its first vehicle, the DAT, in 1914. The company was renamed Kaishinsha Motorcar Co., Ltd. in 1918 and DAT Jidosha & Co., Ltd. in 1925, before its 1926 merger into DAT Jidosha Seizo Co., Ltd.

claim: Kwaishinsha Motor Car Works was established by Masujiro Hashimoto on 1 July 1911 in Tokyo's Azabu-Hiroo district. [evidence: n-70f20d90]
