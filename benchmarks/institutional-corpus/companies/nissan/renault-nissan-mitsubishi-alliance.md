# Renault-Nissan-Mitsubishi Alliance

Renault-Nissan formed 27 March 1999 when Renault bought a 36.8 percent stake in Nissan Motor Co., Ltd. Mitsubishi Motors joined in 2016 when Nissan acquired a 34 percent stake in Mitsubishi. As of November 2023, Renault and Nissan each hold a reciprocal 15 percent stake in the other; Nissan's stake in Mitsubishi was reduced to 24 percent in November 2024.
