# Assertion: Facebook, Inc. is the current legal name (pre-2021)

This note preserves a plain assertion that was true for the period between the 2005 rename and the 28 October 2021 rebrand: that Facebook, Inc. was the current legal entity name. It is kept rather than deleted, and it is now contradicted by meta-platforms-2021, which records the name that superseded it.

claim: Facebook, Inc. is the current legal name of the company that operates Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp. [evidence: n-4bbf0507]
