# Facebook, Inc. (2005 rename)

In August 2005 Mark Zuckerberg dropped "The" from the product and corporate name, becoming Facebook, Inc. This name held until the 2021 rebrand to Meta Platforms.

claim: TheFacebook, Inc. dropped "The" from its name and became Facebook, Inc. on 23 August 2005. [evidence: n-746a6bd5]
