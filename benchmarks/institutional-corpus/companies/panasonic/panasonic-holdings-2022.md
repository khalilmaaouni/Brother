# Panasonic Holdings Corporation (2022 reorganization, current)

Announced in November 2020, Panasonic converted its operating divisions into wholly owned subsidiaries and became a holding company named Panasonic Holdings Corporation on 1 April 2022. This is the current legal name as of 2026.
