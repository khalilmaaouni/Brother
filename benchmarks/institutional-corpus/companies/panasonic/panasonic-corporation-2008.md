# Panasonic Corporation (2008 rename)

The rename to Panasonic Corporation was announced 10 January 2008, approved by shareholders 26 June 2008, and took effect 1 October 2008, unifying the company under its global brand name.

claim: Matsushita Electric Industrial Co., Ltd. was renamed Panasonic Corporation effective 1 October 2008. [evidence: n-e6cd52d9]
