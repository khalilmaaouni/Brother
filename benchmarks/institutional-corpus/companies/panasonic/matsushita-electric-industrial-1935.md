# Matsushita Electric Industrial Co., Ltd. (1935 incorporation)

Originally founded in 1918 as Matsushita Electric Housewares Manufacturing Works, the business was incorporated as Matsushita Electric Industrial Co., Ltd. in December 1935. This name held for over 70 years before the 2008 rename to Panasonic Corporation.

claim: Konosuke Matsushita incorporated his sole proprietorship as Matsushita Electric Industrial Co., Ltd. in December 1935. [evidence: n-83632a71]
