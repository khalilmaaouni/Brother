# Sony Interactive Entertainment (2016 formation)

Sony Interactive Entertainment LLC formed 1 April 2016 by merging Sony Computer Entertainment with Sony Network Entertainment International, headquartered in San Mateo, California. It is the PlayStation business subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation.
