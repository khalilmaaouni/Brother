# Tokyo Tsushin Kogyo K.K. (1946)

Sony's founding legal entity. Masaru Ibuka and Akio Morita established Tokyo Tsushin Kogyo K.K. in Tokyo on 7 May 1946. The company later renamed itself Sony Corporation.
