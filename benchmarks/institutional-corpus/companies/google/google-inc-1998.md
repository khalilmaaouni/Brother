# Google Inc. (1998 incorporation)

Larry Page and Sergey Brin incorporated Google Inc. on 4 September 1998. The company was reorganized under Alphabet Inc. in 2015 and converted to Google LLC in 2017.

claim: Google Inc. was formally incorporated on 4 September 1998 in Menlo Park, California. [evidence: n-14038cb6]
