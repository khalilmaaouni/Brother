# Toyota Motor Company Ltd. (1937 spinoff, current lineage)

Toyota Motor Company Ltd. began trading 28 August 1937 as an independent company, spun off from the Automobile Division of Toyoda Automatic Loom Works. The founding family's name, Toyoda, was deliberately respelled Toyota for the new company. This lineage continues today as Toyota Motor Corporation, which remains a separate legal entity from Toyota Industries Corporation (the renamed Toyoda Automatic Loom Works).
