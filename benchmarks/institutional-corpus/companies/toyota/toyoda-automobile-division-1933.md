# Toyoda Automatic Loom Works, Automobile Division (1933)

An internal division of Toyoda Automatic Loom Works, established 1 September 1933 to develop automobiles. Spun off as the independent Toyota Motor Company Ltd. on 28 August 1937.

claim: Toyoda Automatic Loom Works established an Automobile Division on 1 September 1933. [evidence: n-c4867069]
