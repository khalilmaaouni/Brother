# Toyoda Automatic Loom Works, Ltd. (1926)

Founded by Sakichi Toyoda to manufacture the automatic looms he invented. Still exists today as Toyota Industries Corporation. In 1933 it established an Automobile Division, which was spun off in 1937 as the independent Toyota Motor Company Ltd.

claim: Sakichi Toyoda incorporated Toyoda Automatic Loom Works, Ltd. in Kariya, Japan; the incorporation meeting was held 17 November 1926 and the establishment was registered 18 November 1926. [evidence: n-c9a7d4ee]
